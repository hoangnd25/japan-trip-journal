Alps & classic cities

Tokyo → Takayama → Kyoto → Osaka

Our first pick

The most varied trip: a traditional mountain town between cities we already enjoy. Takayama brings old streets, morning markets and Hida beef; Kyoto and Osaka can be relaxed revisits.

7 days · 6 nights

  1. Tokyo2 nights
  2. Takayama2 nights
  3. Kyoto1 night
  4. Osaka1 night

Time for one or two favourite neighbourhoods in Tokyo, two slower nights in Takayama, then a short Kansai finish.

With 9–10 days

Add Shirakawa-go on the way to Kanazawa. Use Osaka as the final base, with Kyoto as a day trip.

10 days

The trade-off: more hotel changes, plus a substantial journey from Tokyo to Takayama. Keep transfer days light; allow flexibility for mountain weather.

Hokkaido & Tokyo

Sapporo → Hakodate → Tokyo

Choose this if snowy scenery is the big draw. Sapporo brings ramen, soup curry and winter play; Otaru adds canals and music boxes; Hakodate brings markets and harbour views. No ski itinerary needed.

Otaru is a day trip from Sapporo — no extra hotel change.

7 days · 6 nights

  1. Sapporo3 nights
  2. Hakodate1 night
  3. Tokyo2 nights

Stay in Sapporo for city exploring and the Otaru day trip, then a brief Hakodate stop before familiar favourites in Tokyo.

With 9–10 days

Give Hakodate a full day between transfers. A tenth day adds more breathing room in Sapporo.

9 days
Sapporo 3 · Hakodate 2 · Tokyo 3
10 days
Sapporo 4 · Hakodate 2 · Tokyo 3

Numbers are nights in each place.

The trade-off: the 7-day version puts two major travel days back to back. Nine days is more comfortable. Compare train and flight options for the Hakodate–Tokyo leg.

Fukuoka to Tokyo

Fukuoka → Osaka → Tokyo

The straightforward, food-focused choice. Start somewhere new with Fukuoka’s ramen and yatai food stalls, then revisit Osaka and Tokyo along the main rail corridor.

Kyoto is a day trip from Osaka — keep one Kansai hotel base.

7 days · 6 nights

  1. Fukuoka2 nights
  2. Osaka2 nights
  3. Tokyo2 nights

A balanced city trip, with Kyoto from Osaka and an optional Dazaifu outing from Fukuoka.

With 9–10 days

Add Hiroshima for two nights, including a Miyajima day trip. A tenth day gives Kansai more time.

9 days
Fukuoka 2 · Hiroshima 2 · Osaka 2 · Tokyo 2
10 days

The trade-off: mostly cities, with less small-town contrast. The rail route is simple to follow, but the journeys still take a sizeable part of a day.

Before we book

These are starting-point plans. Confirm flights and train or bus connections before choosing the route. In February, Hokkaido is the strongest snow option; snow in the Alps varies. City photos show evening scenes rather than promise snow or blossoms.

Winter festival photos illustrate the season, not a confirmed event in our dates. Check the travel year’s event dates before adding a festival.
