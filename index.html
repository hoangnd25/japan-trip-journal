Fukuoka → Osaka → Tokyo

Fukuoka’s ramen and yatai food stalls, then Osaka and Tokyo along the main rail corridor. Add Dazaifu as a Fukuoka day trip.

Kyoto is a day trip from Osaka — keep one Kansai hotel base.
