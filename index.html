Big days for kids

A Disney day fits any route’s Tokyo stay. Osaka adds the option of Universal Studios Japan. Choose around favourite characters and leave a full day for the park.

Tokyo DisneySea

Tokyo · any route · full day

Frozen Kingdom and Rapunzel’s Forest make this a strong option for Frozen and Tangled fans. Mermaid Lagoon adds colourful indoor attractions and Ariel’s Playground for exploring between rides.

Make room for it: a two-night Tokyo stay usually leaves one full day. Use that day for DisneySea instead of city sightseeing, or add a Tokyo night on a longer trip.

Tokyo Disneyland

Tokyo · alternative to DisneySea

Pooh’s Hunny Hunt, “it’s a small world” and Toontown offer familiar characters and playful settings. Worth comparing with DisneySea if classic Disney stories are the bigger draw.

Choose one park: allow a full day here too. For a seven-day trip, picking Disneyland or DisneySea keeps room for the rest of the route.

Universal Studios Japan

Osaka · Alps or Fukuoka route · full day

Super Nintendo World brings Mario and Yoshi into the day, alongside Minion Park. Check ride height requirements; Nintendo area entry may need a timed ticket.

Adjust the Osaka stay: the Alps plan needs an extra Osaka night or a reshuffle. On the Fukuoka route, swap out the Kyoto day trip or add a night.

Before booking, check ride suitability and height limits: Disney parks · Universal Studios Japan.

Option1

Alps & classic cities

Mountain-town charm & classic cities

A first visit to Takayama brings old streets, local crafts and Hida beef. Continue to Kyoto and Osaka for a return to familiar city favourites.

What to expect

For the 7-day route

The experience
A first visit to Takayama adds traditional streets and mountain scenery between familiar city stays. Hida beef and local ramen bring new flavours before Kyoto sweets and Osaka street food.
For kids
Sarubobo doll-making, Kyoto Railway Museum and Kaiyukan add hands-on activities and indoor outings. For Mario or Minion fans, Universal Studios Japan is a full-day option if the Osaka stay is extended or the plan reshuffled.
Pace & planning
Four bases and three hotel changes in six nights, with one night each in Kyoto and Osaka. Tokyo–Takayama and Takayama–Kyoto are substantial transfer days, so leave room for connections, luggage and breaks.

Activities & attractions

Old towns, traditional crafts and city walks.

Option2

Hokkaido

Snow, canal towns & northern cuisine

Hokkaido offers a change of scenery from Japan’s major cities: snow play, Otaru’s canal and music-box shops, and Hakodate’s harbour. Regional ramen, soup curry and seafood round out the route.

What to expect

For the 7-day route

The experience
Snowy landscapes, Otaru’s canal and Hakodate’s harbour give the northern stops a distinct feel. Miso ramen, soup curry, sushi and seafood are part of the appeal.
For kids
Snow play and tubing offer an active winter outing, with Shiroi Koibito Park and AOAO aquarium as alternatives. Allow for warm layers, suitable footwear and changes to outdoor plans.
Pace & planning
Three bases and two hotel changes; Otaru is a day trip from Sapporo. The seven-day plan still puts two long journeys back to back, leaving only one night in Hakodate. Nine days adds a second Hakodate night and more time between journeys.

Activities & attractions

Snow play, canal walks and indoor discoveries.

Food highlights

Ramen and soup curry in Sapporo; seafood farther along.

Option3

Fukuoka & Kansai

Kyushu flavours & city day trips

A first visit to Fukuoka combines Dazaifu and Marine World with Hakata ramen and evening food stalls. Continue to Osaka, with Kyoto as a day trip to keep hotel changes manageable.

What to expect

For the 7-day route

The experience
A first visit to Fukuoka brings Hakata ramen, hot pots and local specialities, followed by familiar Osaka and Kyoto. The route is mainly cities, with less small-town scenery and little focus on snow.
For kids
Marine World, the science museum and Ohori Park offer aquarium, hands-on and outdoor options. In Osaka, Universal Studios Japan can replace the Kyoto day trip. Pick a main outing in each city rather than trying to fit everything in.
Pace & planning
Three bases and two hotel changes along the main Shinkansen corridor, with Kyoto as an Osaka day trip. Fukuoka–Osaka and Osaka–Tokyo still take a sizeable part of two days once station access and check-in are included.

Activities & attractions

Shrine gardens, marine life and familiar city stops.

Food highlights

Hakata ramen in Fukuoka, then familiar Osaka favourites.

Planning notes

Activities are suggestions, not a daily checklist. Confirm dates, flights and train or bus connections before committing. Seven days means six nights, including arrival and departure. Check activity age requirements and availability.

Hokkaido offers the strongest snow focus; conditions in the Japanese Alps vary. Otaru’s photo features winter festival lights; confirm event dates before planning a visit. Fuji is cover inspiration, not a scheduled stop.
