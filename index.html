Sapporo → Otaru → Hakodate → Tokyo

Ramen steam on a cold day. Music boxes in Otaru. A harbour city and snowy playtime. A northern adventure with plenty of small delights.

BEFORE WE BOOK

Winter is a possibility.

The shortlist suggests a winter trip. Dates are still open: snow and activities depend on the season and weather. Hokkaido is the strongest winter choice.

Keep travel days light.

These are suggested overnight allocations. Arrival and departure days are included. Confirm flights, rail and bus connections before booking; exact journey times and fares aren’t included.
